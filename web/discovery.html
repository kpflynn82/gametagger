THE FIVE PILLARS

What a platform has to get right

Discovery is not one feature. It is five jobs that depend on each other, and most platforms are strong at one or two. For each job, here is what I believe and what I have done about it.

01 · RECOMMENDATIONS

Know what is inside every game

Recommendations can only be as good as the labels under them. A store that knows a game as "Puzzle" cannot tell a quick match-3 break from a game with a guild, a battle pass and an energy timer, so it cannot match either one to the right player.

WHAT I HAVE DONE
GameTagger. Tags top mobile and Steam games against 189 gameplay tags and 100 genres. One AI describes only what it can see, a second decides each tag on its own, and anything unproven stays marked unknown. gametagger.vercel.app
Xbox AI Discovery Queue. Built the AI Discovery Queue at Xbox.
02 · QUALITY

Keep low-quality content out of the way

Every slot given to a low-effort game is a slot taken from one a player would love. Quality has to be checked before ranking starts. It cannot be left for players to sort out afterward.

WHAT I HAVE DONE
Quality filter at Xbox. Worked on a quality filter for game discovery at Xbox. The details belong to Xbox. The principle carries to any large catalog, and I am glad to talk it through.
03 · DEVELOPERS

Teach developers what works

Filtering and ranking only sort the catalog you already have. The platform sees what keeps players and what earns, and most developers do not. Sharing that is the cheapest way to get better games to recommend.

WHAT I HAVE DONE
Google Play launch consultations. Created a launch consultation growth report that drove $200M in incremental revenue in its first year and $250M in its second. Tuned at least 30 mobile game economies from top to bottom.
Teardowns and Roblox Radar. Tools that work out why a rising game works and turn it into lessons a developer can use. More on both below.
Talks. Mobile Game Launch Best Practices at GDC, and Google Playtime sessions in Milan, London and São Paulo.
04 · SEARCH

Make search find the game

A player who types what they want should get it, whether they type it into the store or into Google. Search is the one moment a player tells you exactly what they are looking for.

WHAT I HAVE DONE
Xbox search visibility. Fixed a 15-year-old search problem at Xbox in weeks, using AI tools I built. Top-10 visibility on Google rose 64%.
05 · BEYOND THE STORE

Meet players where they already watch

A lot of discovery happens before a player opens the store: on a stream, in a clip, from a creator they trust. A platform that only works on its own shelves is working on the last step.

WHAT I HAVE DONE
Streamer-Genre Intelligence. Finds mid-size streamers on Twitch, Kick and YouTube who already play a game's genre and can be reached for a launch. streamer-genre-dashboard.vercel.app

PILLAR 05

Streamer-Genre Intelligence

A launch team's question is "who should we send this game to?" This dashboard answers it by genre. It covers Twitch, Kick and YouTube, refreshes weekly, and gives each streamer an outreach score from 0 to 100 based on genre fit, the variety of their recent games, audience size, language and whether they can be reached. The sweet spot is 500 to 5,000 average viewers: large enough to matter, small enough to answer.

PILLAR 03

Roblox Radar and teardowns

Roblox Radar pulls Roblox's public data every six hours and turns it into a weekly digest: the biggest movers, what the new breakouts have in common, and which trends are fading. The teardowns go deeper on single games: study a hit from what anyone can observe, write down why it works, then design an original game that improves on it. Evolve, don't copy. One teardown became Hoard & Hatch, a playable dice-board prototype built in about a day.
